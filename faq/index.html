General Questions

How does it work?

We set up a phone consult to see if we are a good fit and if naturopathic medicine will help you. An intake is scheduled, where we go over your health history in detail.

I will look at labs at that time and decide if we need additional labs. Labs are cash pay or you can ask your PCP to order them for you.  A custom treatment plan is created  to guide you in moving forward and making health decisions that will restore your vitality. 

We meet to go over the plan and make decisions together that feel comfortable for you. Improving your health naturally sometimes involves detours and setbacks, and these are crucial points of information that help us realign your daily decisions so you can meet your health goals. Two follow up appointments are included and scheduled in, to help you meet your goals.

After the package is finished, you have the choice how to move forward. The most successful people that I have worked with are the ones who are committed to their health, able to make changes, consistently show up and understand that healing takes time. Ongoing regular appointments support your healing journey.

What to expect?

Initial appointment is 1.5-2hrs, depending on how complicated things are. Follow up appointments are 60 minutes.

Do you treat ______?

Probably, yes. My training as a naturopathic doctor is as a primary care doctor. So I generally treat most issues. This is great, because the body is all connected, none of the systems are exclusive to one another. You cannot treat the thyroid or the brain without treating the gut. However, in my practice so far, I have encountered many patients with a condition called Ehler’s Danlos or Collagen Disorder syndrome.

So I have expertise in that area. This actually affects every single system in the body, because the collagen makes up all fascia, protein, holds the skeleton and organs together and is involved everywhere in the body’s systems.

What is Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome (EDS)?

Ehlers-Danlos syndrome (EDS) are a group of inherited genetic conditions, which often go undiagnosed for years. Hypermobile EDS is a multi-genetic disease which I believe involves epigenetic changes that are due to stress, trauma and/or environmental toxins.

The nervous system is often dysregulated, which can cause many different symptoms, and may seem to be unrelated. Natural medicine is uniquely poised to deal with this, as we focus on ALL the systems. I especially love connecting the dots and watch the ‘aha’ moments happen when people realize that there are reasons why they are feeling and experiencing what seems inexplicable.

What is Naturopathic Medicine?

Naturopathic medicine is a practice that uses holistic practices and natural supplements rather than pharmaceuticals to help solve health challenges. In that manner, I am most like a primary care doctor – working with you one-on-one to support your health.

What is Acupuncture?

Acupuncture is a way of addressing health issues based on ancient Chinese practices. It works to return a body to health by achieving balance.

Does Acupuncture hurt?

The needles used in acupuncture are smaller in diameter than a human hair. Sometimes you might feel a slight pinch, but very rarely is it painful at all.

Do you take insurance?

Insurance is not accepted at this time for any services. As an out-of-network provider for acupuncture only, I will provide a superbill that you may submit to your insurance company. Payment is up front at the time of service.

Are you licensed?

The term naturopath is unregulated and may be used by anyone promoting natural therapies with or without clinical education.

A Naturopathic Doctor is an individual who has successfully graduated from one of the 7 accredited medical programs in North America and is eligible to sit for Naturopathic medical boards (NPLEX).

A Naturopathic Physician is a Naturopathic Doctor who holds a license to practice medicine in one of the 21 licensed states.

What are your credentials?

Nadene holds a Doctorate of Naturopathic Medicine from the National University of Health Sciences in Lombard, IL. She has passed the national board exams (NPLEX) which allows her to obtain a license from a state where naturopathic doctors are licensed.

She holds a Master of Science in Acupuncture and is a Licensed Acupuncturist in the state of Illinois. She also is a Licensed Massage Therapist in the state of Illinois.
