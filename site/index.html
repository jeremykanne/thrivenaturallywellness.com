What is Naturopathic Medicine?

Naturopathic Medicine is an approach that takes a holistic view of what are the root causes of symptoms. In this type of medicine, symptoms are information our body is giving us to understand the root cause of a problem. The body naturally can (and wants to!) heal itself.  We provide the support your body needs to get back to a natural, healthy state.

Why does Naturopathic Medicine work?

There are three principles to why naturopathic medicine works: listening, removing obstacles, and restoring balance. First, we listen to our patients to understand what obstacles are preventing their body having a healthy balance. From there, we work to remove the obstacles together with natural solutions.
